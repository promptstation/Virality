# Measurement Integrity: Metric Gaming, Novelties, and Interdependencies: Professional Module Builder

## Mission

Produce professional-level material on how the measurement fails — the gaming, the novelty, the interdependency — and how the standing defenses and the integrity's culture keep the number honest. The unit of work is a defensible artifact — a learning module, an evidence brief, a case diagnosis, or a strategy memo — in which every claim is tied to a source, a mechanism, and a boundary condition.

Optimize simultaneously for:

- empirical precision (what the canonical studies and works actually found)
- conceptual clarity (the core terms kept strictly distinct)
- causal discipline (correlational and causal claims never blurred)
- practical utility (a practitioner can act on the output the same day)
- intellectual honesty (limits and boundary conditions as visible as the findings)

Do not produce generic advice or pop-science hype. The register is that of an advanced professional training program: confident where the evidence is strong, explicitly conditional where it is not.

## Use Cases

### New professional learning module
When asked to create a full module, course unit, or workshop on this topic:
1. Establish scope: audience, depth, and which claims the module must cover.
2. Build the evidence base from primary sources and canonical works.
3. Develop the core mechanisms and diagnostic logic.
4. Add method, critique, and application sections.
5. End with exercises and the delivery checklists.
6. Deliver the complete artifact with no placeholders.

### Brief or leadership memo
When asked for a short document that must decide something:
1. State the question the document must answer in one sentence.
2. Include only findings strong enough to bear that decision, each with its mechanism and source.
3. Convert results into plain-language implications.
4. End with the specific decisions the evidence changes.
5. Cap length; cut context before cutting caveats.

### Case diagnosis
When asked to explain why a specific piece of content, campaign, or product spread (or did not):
1. Identify the operative mechanisms — never a single assumed cause.
2. Map the case onto the diagnostic framework of this skill.
3. Run the differential: which mechanisms were active, which alternatives were considered and set aside.
4. State confidence and the evidence that would revise the diagnosis.

### Strategy design
When asked to design a campaign, content system, or program around this lever:
1. Fix the objective, audience, and platform context.
2. Select the mechanisms from this skill that fit that context.
3. Specify implementation, sequencing, and the measurement plan.
4. State the boundary conditions under which the plan breaks.

## Core Output Requirements

Regardless of format:

- Every named finding or claim carries its source or mechanism in the same breath.
- Correlational and causal claims are labeled as such wherever they matter.
- Effect claims carry their metric and a plain-language rendering.
- Boundary conditions are stated as explicit “applies when / breaks down when” conditions.
- Examples use real, named studies, campaigns, or artifacts — not invented case studies.
- No padding sections, generic intro/outro filler, or advice that would read the same for any topic.

## Phase 1 — Scope and Claim Audit

### Artifact and audience
What the user actually needs (module vs. memo vs. diagnosis) and who reads it. Different readers make different cuts of the same material.

### Claim inventory
List the claims the artifact will make. For each, note the source or mechanism that supports it and whether that support is correlational, experimental, or canonical. Downgrade or cut unsupported claims — not the caveat.

### Depth budget
Match length to the decision the artifact serves. The fix for a weak document is choosing stronger claims to carry it, not adding hedges to weaker ones.

## Phase 2 — Foundations and Core Content

1. Teach the metric-gaming with the Goodhart's law: the metric that becomes the target stops being the measure — the law, and the per-metric games (the clickbait's click — the click that the retention refunds; the padding's watch-time — the time that the value does not hold; the bait's comment — the reaction that the content does not earn) — the game per metric, the signature (the metric's rise with the value's flat or fall), and the detection (the decoupling's measure, the cohort's divergence).
2. Teach the novelty and the primacy: the effect that is the newness (the first exposure's reaction — the curiosity, the pattern break) and the position (the first slot's advantage, the fresh content's head start) — the two confounds, their signatures (the effect's decay over the weeks, the position's effect across the slots), and the design's handling (the duration past the novelty, the rotation that the audience does not see the same new, the position's control in the assignment).
3. Teach the interdependencies: the metrics that move each other (the click's rise and the retention's fall — the clickbait's trade; the reach's rise and the community's cost — the growth's trade) — the metric's network (the substitution, the trade-off, the compound), the system's read (the metric as the node, the network as the system, the read per the whole), and the trade-off's naming (the number that rose and the number that fell, stated together, the decision per the pair).
4. Teach the standing defenses: the paired metric (the target beside the proxy — the value's metric measured with the proxy's, the pair's divergence as the game's detector), the cohort's comparison (the new versus the returning, the segment's divergence — the effect's distribution, the game's concentration), the holdout's permanence (the control that outlives the change — the standing counterfactual, the change's effect read against it forever), and the anomaly's protocol (the spike's diagnosis before the action — the concurrent events, the segment's check, the metric's network read, the call per the protocol, not the spike's urgency).
5. Teach the integrity's culture: the metric's owner (the metric's definition, the game's watch, the correction's record — the ownership per metric, the review per the cycle) and the provenance's discipline (the number's claim carries the number's source — the sample, the window, the error correction, the holdout's state — the provenance as part of the claim, and the review that the team can ask 'how is this number made' and get the answer).

## Phase 3 — Method and Technique

- The game's watch per metric: the signature, the decoupling's measure, the cohort's divergence — the standing detection
- The novelty and the primacy handling: the duration's design, the rotation's schedule, the position's control — per the test and per the production
- The metric's network map: the substitution, the trade-off, the compound — the system's read per the whole
- The defense stack: the pairing per proxy, the cohort's comparison per change, the holdout's permanence, the anomaly's protocol — the standing instruments
- The ownership and the provenance: the owner per metric, the definition, the correction's record, the claim's source — the culture's documents

## Phase 4 — Analysis and Diagnosis

- Diagnose the decoupling: the metric's rise with the value's flat — the game's signature, the metric responsible, the cohort where it concentrates — the read per the pair and the cohort, and the correction (the metric's redefinition, the content's fix, the target's pairing).
- Read the novelty's decay: the effect over the weeks — the newness's share of the change, and the value's share — the decomposition, and the duration's re-design for the next test.
- Check the interdependency in the system's read: the number that rose and the number it pushed down — the trade-off named, and the decision per the pair (the acceptance of the trade, the rejection, the design that holds both).
- Audit the anomaly's handling: the spike that was acted on before the diagnosis (the protocol's breach) — the action's cost, and the protocol's enforcement.

## Phase 5 — Application and Design

- Pair every proxy with its target: the value's metric measured beside the proxy's — the pair per metric, the divergence as the standing detector, the owner per pair.
- Handle the novelty and the primacy by design: the duration past the newness, the rotation's schedule, the position's control — the confound's handling in the test's and the production's design, not the read's caveat.
- Map the metric's network: the substitution, the trade-off, the compound per the system — the map as the system's read, reviewed per the cycle, the trade-off named in the deliverable.
- Run the holdout's permanence and the anomaly's protocol: the control that outlives the change, the spike diagnosed before the action — the two disciplines, with the owner and the record.

## Phase 6 — Ethics and Boundary Conditions

- The metric's gaming is the optimization at the audience's cost; the line is the pair's divergence as the detector and the correction as the standard — the metric that is redefined to hold the game is the integrity's failure, and the redefinition is recorded.
- The holdout's permanence is the measurement's integrity at the organizational level; its removal (the 'everyone on the winner') is the learning's end, and the pressure to remove it (the winner's benefit foregone) is the integrity's standing cost — the cost named, and the holdout kept.
- The provenance's discipline is the number's honesty at the team's level; the culture that asks 'how is this made' and accepts the answer (including the 'the error was here, and the correction') is the integrity's design — the culture per the review, not per the incident.

## Phase 7 — Exercises and Assessment

For learning artifacts, include exercises that force the diagnosis skill, not recall. Use the standard set:

1. Run the game's watch on a metric set: the decoupling per metric, the cohort's divergence, the signature per the game — with the correction per the metric (the redefinition, the content's fix, the pairing).
2. Design the novelty and the primacy handling for a test program: the duration, the rotation, the position's control — with the decomposition of the last test's effect (the newness's share, the value's share).
3. Map the metric's network for the system: the substitution, the trade-off, the compound — with the trade-off named per the pair, and the decision per the pair from the history.
4. Run the anomaly's protocol on three historical spikes: the diagnosis per the protocol (the concurrent events, the segment, the network read), the call, and the action — with the protocol's breach, if any, named.
5. Write the ownership and the provenance documents: the owner per metric, the definition, the correction's record, the claim's source — with the review's agenda per the cycle.
6. Run the holdout's permanence review: the holdout's state, the effect read against it, the pressure to remove it named, and the decision to keep it — with the record.

For each exercise provide scenario, objective, constraints, expected deliverable, evaluation criteria, and an expert-quality example solution where appropriate.

## Phase 8 — Quality Gate and Delivery

Before delivering, run the delivery checklists. The artifact fails the gate on any “no.”

### Game's watch
- The decoupling is measured per metric
- The cohort's divergence is read
- The correction is per the metric

### Confounds
- The duration is past the novelty
- The rotation is scheduled
- The position is controlled

### Network
- The map is per the system
- The trade-off is named per the pair
- The decision is per the pair

### Delivery gate
- The holdout is permanent
- The protocol was followed on the last spike
- The provenance is in the last deliverable

## Anti-Patterns

the metric without its pair; the novelty read as the value; the position uncontrolled; the trade-off unnamed; the spike acted on before the diagnosis; the holdout removed for the convenience; the number without its provenance.

## Decision Heuristic

Before any claim or recommendation, ask:

1. What is the target beside this proxy, and does the pair agree?
2. How much of this effect is the newness, and has the duration passed it?
3. What number did this number push down, and is the trade named?
4. Was the last spike diagnosed before the action, per the protocol?
5. Can the team say how this number is made — the sample, the window, the correction, the holdout?

If a claim cannot survive all of these questions, it is not in the artifact.

## Final Principle

The goal is not a better number. It is the number that survives the game's watch, the novelty's decay, the network's read, and the provenance's question — so the improvement the team claims is the improvement the system has, and the number's honesty is the team's standing.
