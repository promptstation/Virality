Go beyond the before-after chart as the causal evidence, and the correlation as the mechanism and examine each method as the counterfactual's construction — the untreated's path, the treated's path, the difference as the effect — and the assumption as the claim's load-bearing wall.

Teach the causal ladder: the first rung (the association — what co-moves with what), the second (the action — what happens when the variable is set), and the third (the counterfactual — what would have happened otherwise) — the rungs, and the discipline that the strategy's claim is bounded by the rung the evidence supports (the association's evidence does not carry the counterfactual's claim).

Teach the interrupted time series: the before-after across the event (the platform's change, the policy's shift), the level and the trend (the step and the slope, per the pre-period's pattern), and the assumption (the pre-period's trend is the counterfactual's trend — the no-other-change examination, the concurrent events' naming) — the ITS as the first quasi-method, and its claim's boundary (the effect since the event, given the trend held).

Teach the difference-in-differences: the treated and the untreated across the event, the parallel-trends assumption (the two groups' pre-period trends are the counterfactual's evidence), and the examination (the pre-trend's plot, the placebo's test — the event's date shifted, the effect's absence) — the DiD as the treated's effect from the untreated's path, and its claim's boundary (the effect on the treated, given the trends were parallel).

Teach the synthetic control: the constructed untreated (the comparators' weighted match to the treated's pre-period), the counterfactual's path (the synthetic's post-period), and the inference (the permutation test — the treated's gap versus the comparators' gaps, the rank as the significance) — the synthetic as the counterfactual's construction, and its claim's boundary (the effect on the treated, given the comparators' pool and the match).

Teach the instrumental variable: the instrument (the variable that moves the treatment without moving the outcome — the relevance and the exclusion), the local effect (the compliers' effect, not the population's), and the read (the first stage, the second stage, the excluded-variance bound) — the IV as the endogeneity's cure, and its claim's boundary (the compliers' effect, given the instrument's validity).
