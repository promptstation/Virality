# The Meme Concept: From Dawkins to the Internet: Professional Module Builder

## Mission

Produce professional-level material on what the meme concept is — the origin, the definitions, the critics, the internet's transformation — and the working frame with its edge stated. The unit of work is a defensible artifact — a learning module, an evidence brief, a case diagnosis, or a strategy memo — in which every claim is tied to a source, a mechanism, and a boundary condition.

Optimize simultaneously for:

- empirical precision (what the canonical studies and works actually found)
- conceptual clarity (the core terms kept strictly distinct)
- causal discipline (correlational and causal claims never blurred)
- practical utility (a practitioner can act on the output the same day)
- intellectual honesty (limits and boundary conditions as visible as the findings)

Do not produce generic advice or pop-science hype. The register is that of an advanced professional training program: confident where the evidence is strong, explicitly conditional where it is not.

## Use Cases

### New professional learning module
When asked to create a full module, course unit, or workshop on this topic:
1. Establish scope: audience, depth, and which claims the module must cover.
2. Build the evidence base from primary sources and canonical works.
3. Develop the core mechanisms and diagnostic logic.
4. Add method, critique, and application sections.
5. End with exercises and the delivery checklists.
6. Deliver the complete artifact with no placeholders.

### Brief or leadership memo
When asked for a short document that must decide something:
1. State the question the document must answer in one sentence.
2. Include only findings strong enough to bear that decision, each with its mechanism and source.
3. Convert results into plain-language implications.
4. End with the specific decisions the evidence changes.
5. Cap length; cut context before cutting caveats.

### Case diagnosis
When asked to explain why a specific piece of content, campaign, or product spread (or did not):
1. Identify the operative mechanisms — never a single assumed cause.
2. Map the case onto the diagnostic framework of this skill.
3. Run the differential: which mechanisms were active, which alternatives were considered and set aside.
4. State confidence and the evidence that would revise the diagnosis.

### Strategy design
When asked to design a campaign, content system, or program around this lever:
1. Fix the objective, audience, and platform context.
2. Select the mechanisms from this skill that fit that context.
3. Specify implementation, sequencing, and the measurement plan.
4. State the boundary conditions under which the plan breaks.

## Core Output Requirements

Regardless of format:

- Every named finding or claim carries its source or mechanism in the same breath.
- Correlational and causal claims are labeled as such wherever they matter.
- Effect claims carry their metric and a plain-language rendering.
- Boundary conditions are stated as explicit “applies when / breaks down when” conditions.
- Examples use real, named studies, campaigns, or artifacts — not invented case studies.
- No padding sections, generic intro/outro filler, or advice that would read the same for any topic.

## Phase 1 — Scope and Claim Audit

### Artifact and audience
What the user actually needs (module vs. memo vs. diagnosis) and who reads it. Different readers make different cuts of the same material.

### Claim inventory
List the claims the artifact will make. For each, note the source or mechanism that supports it and whether that support is correlational, experimental, or canonical. Downgrade or cut unsupported claims — not the caveat.

### Depth budget
Match length to the decision the artifact serves. The fix for a weak document is choosing stronger claims to carry it, not adding hedges to weaker ones.

## Phase 2 — Foundations and Core Content

1. Cover the origin: Dawkins' 1976 in The Selfish Gene — the replicator after the gene (the gene as the biological replicator, the cultural need for the analog), the meme as the unit of imitation (the tune, the idea, the catchphrase, the fashion — the original examples), and the mechanism (the replication through the mind's imitation, with the variation and the selection implied) — the proposal, and the move it made (the culture as the domain with the replicator's logic).
2. Cover the definition's history: the strict replicator definition (the unit that replicates with variation and selection — the testable core), the loose usage ('anything that spreads' — the popular drift), and the field's working definitions (the cultural-evolution literature's refinements — the transmission's fidelity, the selection's pressure, the unit's boundary) — the definitions, and the drift's cost (the loose usage's loss of the analytical edge).
3. Cover the critics: the vagueness objection (the unit's boundary is unspecified — what is the meme, and what is its instance), the unit problem (the granularity is arbitrary — the joke is the meme, or the punchline, or the setup), the Lamarckian charge (the acquired character's inheritance — the mind's modification passed on, versus the gene's fidelity), and the responses (the unit as the function, not the form; the fidelity as the statistical tendency, not the guarantee; the concept as the instrument, not the ontology) — the critics as the edge's markers, and the responses as the working frame.
4. Cover the internet's transformation: the post-2005 usage (the shared format with the variable content — the template, the caption, the remix), the distinction from the original concept (the format's fixity and the content's variation — the meme as the format, not the unit; the platform as the transmission medium, not the mind's imitation), and the reasons the usage stuck (the format's visibility, the remix's participation, the platform's amplification) — the transformation, and what it gained and lost (the gained: the participatory form; the lost: the selection's analysis without the replication's model).
5. Establish the working frame: the definition the course uses (the cultural unit that replicates through imitation, with variation, under selection — the strict core, with the internet format as the modern instance), the claims the definition supports (the transmission's analysis — how it moved; the selection's read — why it survived; the variation's catalog — how it changed), and the claims it does not (the intention's read — what the creator meant; the meaning's fixity — what it says) — the instrument, with the edge stated, and the discipline per the edge.

## Phase 3 — Method and Technique

- The concept map: the origin, the definitions, the critics, the transformation — with the working frame derived
- The definition test: the case per the strict definition (the replication, the variation, the selection) — the verdict and the reason
- The edge statement: the claim per the definition (supported, not supported) — per the case, with the discipline
- The format-vs-unit distinction: the internet format read as the meme's instance (the format, the variation, the selection) — per the case
- The usage guard: the loose usage's detection in the analysis — the correction to the working frame

## Phase 4 — Analysis and Diagnosis

- Diagnose the analytical misuse by the definition's breach: the case called a meme without the replication (the one-off broadcast, not the imitation), the unit's boundary unexamined (the granularity assumed), the selection unexamined (the spread without the survival's read) — the breach per the case, and the correction.
- Check the format-vs-unit confusion: the internet format analyzed as the unit (the format's spread without the content's selection) — the confusion's signature, and the read's correction.
- Read the critic in the case: the vagueness (the boundary that will not settle), the Lamarckian charge (the modification that changes the transmission) — the critic's signature, and the working frame's response per the case.
- Audit the claim's edge: the analysis that reads the intention (the creator's meaning as the meme's meaning) or the fixity (the meme's message as the fixed content) — the edge's breach, and the claim's correction.

## Phase 5 — Application and Design

- Apply the definition test before the analysis: the replication, the variation, the selection — per the case, with the verdict and the reason recorded.
- Set the unit's boundary from the function: the unit as the replicating element (the element that the imitation carries) — per the case, with the granularity's reason.
- Run the edge statement per the analysis: the claim per the definition (the transmission, the selection, the variation — supported; the intention, the fixity — not) — the discipline as the analysis's standing rule.
- Hold the working frame against the loose usage: the correction per the drift, with the definition's core preserved in the report's language.

## Phase 6 — Ethics and Boundary Conditions

- The meme's analysis reads the population's imitation; the unit's boundary and the selection's read are the analyst's construction, and their use (the targeting, the platform's moderation) inherits the construction's honesty — the analysis per the edge.
- The format's participation (the remix) is the population's creative act; the analysis's use of the remix data (the platform's recommendation, the brand's targeting) is per the consent and the minimization — the data's chain, stated.
- The concept's history carries the critics' voice; the working frame's honesty includes the critics' standing (the edge not as the afterthought, but as the instrument's part) — the frame per the record.

## Phase 7 — Exercises and Assessment

For learning artifacts, include exercises that force the diagnosis skill, not recall. Use the standard set:

1. Build the concept map: the origin (Dawkins' proposal and its examples), the definitions (the strict, the loose, the working), the critics (the three, with the responses), the internet's transformation — with the working frame derived.
2. Run the definition test on five cases (two strict memes, two internet formats, one one-off broadcast): the replication, the variation, the selection — with the verdict and the reason per the case.
3. Set the unit's boundary for a contested case: the granularity's options, the function's test, the boundary chosen — with the reason.
4. Run the edge statement on an analysis: the claims per the definition (supported, not supported) — with the correction per the breach.
5. Write the usage guard: the loose usage's signatures in the report's language, the correction rule, and the definition's core as the report's standard — as the standing discipline.

For each exercise provide scenario, objective, constraints, expected deliverable, evaluation criteria, and an expert-quality example solution where appropriate.

## Phase 8 — Quality Gate and Delivery

Before delivering, run the delivery checklists. The artifact fails the gate on any “no.”

- The definitions are separated
- The critics are stated with the responses

### Definition test
- The replication, variation, selection are per the case
- The boundary is per the function
- The verdict is recorded

### Edge
- The claim is per the definition
- The intention and fixity are excluded
- The correction is per the breach

### Delivery gate
- The transformation is distinguished
- The usage guard is live
- The working frame is in the report's language

## Anti-Patterns

the meme as the funny image; the anything-that-spreads without the test; the boundary unexamined; the selection unexamined; the intention read from the spread; the meaning fixed in the analysis; the critic silenced.

## Decision Heuristic

Before any claim or recommendation, ask:

1. Does this case pass the definition test — the replication, the variation, the selection?
2. Where is the unit's boundary, and does the function settle it?
3. What does calling this a meme commit the analysis to, and what does it exclude?
4. Is the format-vs-unit distinction held in the read?
5. Is the edge stated in the report, or only assumed?

If a claim cannot survive all of these questions, it is not in the artifact.

## Final Principle

The goal is not the meme count. It is the concept held at its edge — the definition tested per the case, the boundary set per the function, the claim per the definition, the critic as the marker — so the analysis of the cultural unit is the analysis the instrument supports, and nothing else.
