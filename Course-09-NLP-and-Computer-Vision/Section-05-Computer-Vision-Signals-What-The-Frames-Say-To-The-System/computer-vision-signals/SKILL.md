# Computer Vision Signals: What the Frames Say to the System: Professional Module Builder

## Mission

Produce professional-level material on how the frames are read — the tasks, the temporal, the platform's use, the limits — and how the visual is designed as the system's input, with the ethics held. The unit of work is a defensible artifact — a learning module, an evidence brief, a case diagnosis, or a strategy memo — in which every claim is tied to a source, a mechanism, and a boundary condition.

Optimize simultaneously for:

- empirical precision (what the canonical studies and works actually found)
- conceptual clarity (the core terms kept strictly distinct)
- causal discipline (correlational and causal claims never blurred)
- practical utility (a practitioner can act on the output the same day)
- intellectual honesty (limits and boundary conditions as visible as the findings)

Do not produce generic advice or pop-science hype. The register is that of an advanced professional training program: confident where the evidence is strong, explicitly conditional where it is not.

## Use Cases

### New professional learning module
When asked to create a full module, course unit, or workshop on this topic:
1. Establish scope: audience, depth, and which claims the module must cover.
2. Build the evidence base from primary sources and canonical works.
3. Develop the core mechanisms and diagnostic logic.
4. Add method, critique, and application sections.
5. End with exercises and the delivery checklists.
6. Deliver the complete artifact with no placeholders.

### Brief or leadership memo
When asked for a short document that must decide something:
1. State the question the document must answer in one sentence.
2. Include only findings strong enough to bear that decision, each with its mechanism and source.
3. Convert results into plain-language implications.
4. End with the specific decisions the evidence changes.
5. Cap length; cut context before cutting caveats.

### Case diagnosis
When asked to explain why a specific piece of content, campaign, or product spread (or did not):
1. Identify the operative mechanisms — never a single assumed cause.
2. Map the case onto the diagnostic framework of this skill.
3. Run the differential: which mechanisms were active, which alternatives were considered and set aside.
4. State confidence and the evidence that would revise the diagnosis.

### Strategy design
When asked to design a campaign, content system, or program around this lever:
1. Fix the objective, audience, and platform context.
2. Select the mechanisms from this skill that fit that context.
3. Specify implementation, sequencing, and the measurement plan.
4. State the boundary conditions under which the plan breaks.

## Core Output Requirements

Regardless of format:

- Every named finding or claim carries its source or mechanism in the same breath.
- Correlational and causal claims are labeled as such wherever they matter.
- Effect claims carry their metric and a plain-language rendering.
- Boundary conditions are stated as explicit “applies when / breaks down when” conditions.
- Examples use real, named studies, campaigns, or artifacts — not invented case studies.
- No padding sections, generic intro/outro filler, or advice that would read the same for any topic.

## Phase 1 — Scope and Claim Audit

### Artifact and audience
What the user actually needs (module vs. memo vs. diagnosis) and who reads it. Different readers make different cuts of the same material.

### Claim inventory
List the claims the artifact will make. For each, note the source or mechanism that supports it and whether that support is correlational, experimental, or canonical. Downgrade or cut unsupported claims — not the caveat.

### Depth budget
Match length to the decision the artifact serves. The fix for a weak document is choosing stronger claims to carry it, not adding hedges to weaker ones.

5. 6. Teach the visual's ethics: the face's consent (the person in the frame — the consent to the capture, to the display, to the read's use of the face's attribute), the expression's use (the emotion's inference on the person — the use's line, the context's requirement), and the synthetic's line (the deepfake's and the manipulation's — the disclosure per the policy, the generation's boundary, the person's protection) — the three lines, per the content's people and its nature.

## Phase 4 — Analysis and Diagnosis

- Diagnose the visual's read failure by task: the detection's miss (the subject's occlusion, the open-vocabulary's gap), the aesthetic's low read (the preference's mismatch, the homogenization's pressure), the OCR's error (the font, the angle, the quality), the expression's error (the context's absence) — the task per the case, and the fix (the frame's redesign, the model's domain, the human's review).
- Check the thumbnail's signal: the platform's visual read versus the click's behavior — the divergence (the model's preference, the niche's language), and the design per the read and the line.
- Read the quality's state: the resolution, the encoding, the composition — per the platform's floor and the preference, with the niche's visual language as the design's target.
- Audit the synthetic's handling: the detection's state, the disclosure's policy per the platform, the generation's boundary — the line per the content's nature, with the record.

## Phase 5 — Application and Design

- Spec the visual task per the need: the task, the model, the limit — with the design response per the task (the frame's redesign, the review, the domain's model).
- Design the visual input per the niche's language: the subject's clarity, the quality's floor, the temporal's rhythm — with the thumbnail's honesty as the gate.
- Run the platform's visual read as the standing instrument: the thumbnail's signal, the quality's state, the safety's flag — per the content, with the design response per the read.
- Hold the visual ethics as the spec: the face's consent per the people, the expression's use per the context, the synthetic's disclosure per the nature — the three lines, with the record and the review.

## Phase 6 — Ethics and Boundary Conditions

- The face in the frame is the person's attribute at the read's input; the consent to the capture does not carry to the read's use (the detection's output, the training's data, the public's form) — the chain per the use, stated, and the minimization per the need.
- The expression's read is the inference on the person's state; the use's line is the context (the state's read without the context is the speculation) and the person's protection (the state's use on the vulnerable is the targeting, not the care) — the line per the use.
- The synthetic's line is the person's protection: the deepfake's and the manipulation's disclosure per the policy, the generation's boundary per the person depicted, and the detection's state as the game's read — the line per the person, not per the technology.

1. Run the visual task spec on a content type: the tasks (the object, the scene, the aesthetic, the face, the OCR), the model per the scale, the limit per the task — with the design response per the task.
2. Run the temporal read on a video: the motion, the shot, the rhythm — with the highlight's visual input and the clip's design.
3. Run the platform's visual read on a content set: the thumbnail's signal versus the click's behavior, the quality's state per the floor, the safety's flag — with the design response per the read.
4. Design the visual input for a niche: the subject's clarity, the quality's floor, the temporal's design — with the niche's visual language as the target and the thumbnail's honesty as the gate.
5. Write the visual ethics spec for an operation: the face's consent per the people, the expression's use per the context, the synthetic's disclosure per the nature — with the record and the review's cadence.
6. Run the adversarial read on the synthetic: the detection's state on the generated set, the disclosure's policy per the platform, the generation's boundary — with the line's statement.

For each exercise provide scenario, objective, constraints, expected deliverable, evaluation criteria, and an expert-quality example solution where appropriate.

## Anti-Patterns

the frame without the subject's clarity; the thumbnail that misrepresents; the quality below the floor; the aesthetic read as the taste's truth; the expression used without the context; the face's consent to the capture read as the consent to the use; the synthetic undisclosed.

## Decision Heuristic

Before any claim or recommendation, ask:

1. What does the visual say to the system, per the task, and is the limit named?
2. What does the platform's visual read say, and does the design respond to it?
3. Is the quality per the floor and the niche, and is the thumbnail honest?
4. Is the face's consent per the use, and the expression per the context?
5. Is the synthetic disclosed per the policy, and is the boundary per the person?

If a claim cannot survive all of these questions, it is not in the artifact.

## Final Principle

The goal is not the analyzed frame. It is the visual that the system reads as what it is — the subject clear, the quality held, the thumbnail honest — and the read's ethics per the people and the nature, so the visual's signal is the content's truth, and the person in the frame is the line.
