# Platform and Audience Modifiers: Professional Module Builder

## Mission

Produce professional-level material on the modifier — the platform, the domain, the audience, the culture, the public/private — and the read per the modifier, with the low-arousal choice as the case. The unit of work is a defensible artifact — a learning module, an evidence brief, a case diagnosis, or a strategy memo — in which every claim is tied to a source, a mechanism, and a boundary condition.

Optimize simultaneously for:

- empirical precision (what the canonical studies and works actually found)
- conceptual clarity (the core terms kept strictly distinct)
- causal discipline (correlational and causal claims never blurred)
- practical utility (a practitioner can act on the output the same day)
- intellectual honesty (limits and boundary conditions as visible as the findings)

Do not produce generic advice or pop-science hype. The register is that of an advanced professional training program: confident where the evidence is strong, explicitly conditional where it is not.

## Use Cases

### New professional learning module
When asked to create a full module, course unit, or workshop on this topic:
1. Establish scope: audience, depth, and which claims the module must cover.
2. Build the evidence base from primary sources and canonical works.
3. Develop the core mechanisms and diagnostic logic.
4. Add method, critique, and application sections.
5. End with exercises and the delivery checklists.
6. Deliver the complete artifact with no placeholders.

### Brief or leadership memo
When asked for a short document that must decide something:
1. State the question the document must answer in one sentence.
2. Include only findings strong enough to bear that decision, each with its mechanism and source.
3. Convert results into plain-language implications.
4. End with the specific decisions the evidence changes.
5. Cap length; cut context before cutting caveats.

### Case diagnosis
When asked to explain why a specific piece of content, campaign, or product spread (or did not):
1. Identify the operative mechanisms — never a single assumed cause.
2. Map the case onto the diagnostic framework of this skill.
3. Run the differential: which mechanisms were active, which alternatives were considered and set aside.
4. State confidence and the evidence that would revise the diagnosis.

### Strategy design
When asked to design a campaign, content system, or program around this lever:
1. Fix the objective, audience, and platform context.
2. Select the mechanisms from this skill that fit that context.
3. Specify implementation, sequencing, and the measurement plan.
4. State the boundary conditions under which the plan breaks.

## Core Output Requirements

Regardless of format:

- Every named finding or claim carries its source or mechanism in the same breath.
- Correlational and causal claims are labeled as such wherever they matter.
- Effect claims carry their metric and a plain-language rendering.
- Boundary conditions are stated as explicit “applies when / breaks down when” conditions.
- Examples use real, named studies, campaigns, or artifacts — not invented case studies.
- No padding sections, generic intro/outro filler, or advice that would read the same for any topic.

## Phase 1 — Scope and Claim Audit

### Artifact and audience
What the user actually needs (module vs. memo vs. diagnosis) and who reads it. Different readers make different cuts of the same material.

### Claim inventory
List the claims the artifact will make. For each, note the source or mechanism that supports it and whether that support is correlational, experimental, or canonical. Downgrade or cut unsupported claims — not the caveat.

### Depth budget
Match length to the decision the artifact serves. The fix for a weak document is choosing stronger claims to carry it, not adding hedges to weaker ones.

## Phase 2 — Foundations and Core Content

1. Teach the platform modifier: the mechanics per the platform (the short-video's velocity, the retweet's culture, the sharing-to-friends' norm) — the modifier per the platform, with the effect per the mechanics.
2. Teach the domain modifier: the topic per the domain (the political, the consumer, the human-interest) — the modifier per the domain, with the effect per the topic.
3. Teach the audience modifier: the norm per the community (the audience's state, the community's practice) — the modifier per the audience, with the effect per the norm.
4. Teach the culture and the public/private: the context per the culture (the cultural state per the sharing), the nature per the sharing (the public per the audience, the private per the relationship) — the modifier per the context, with the effect per the nature.
5. Teach the strategic case: the usefulness that dominates the emotion (the practical per the case), the low-arousal choice (the trust, the consideration, the brand safety) — the case per the modifier, with the recommendation per the case.

## Phase 4 — Analysis and Diagnosis

- Diagnose the modifier per the case: the platform's mechanics (the velocity per the short-video), the domain's topic (the political per the urgency), the audience's norm (the community per the practice) — the element per the modifier, and the read per the element.
- Check the strategic case: the usefulness that dominates (the practical per the case), the low-arousal choice (the trust per the consideration) — the element per the case, and the recommendation per the case.
- Read the literature's moderator: the effect per the platform, per the domain, per the audience — the element per the moderator, and the refinement per the general rule.

## Phase 5 — Application and Design

- Adapt per the platform: the mechanics per the platform, the strategy per the mechanics — with the read per the platform.
- Adapt per the domain and the audience: the topic per the domain, the norm per the community — with the read per the element.
- Recommend per the case: the high-arousal per the fit, the low-arousal per the choice — with the recommendation per the case.

## Phase 6 — Ethics and Boundary Conditions

- The platform modifier (the mechanics per the platform) is the platform's design per the element; its use (the strategy per the mechanics, the adaptation per the platform) is per the honesty of the platform.
- The audience modifier (the norm per the community) is the community's state per the element; its use (the design per the norm, the adaptation per the community) is per the dignity of the community.
- The low-arousal choice (the trust, the brand safety) is the organization's standing per the element; its honesty (the choice per the case, the safety per the brand) is the record per the case.

## Phase 7 — Exercises and Assessment

For learning artifacts, include exercises that force the diagnosis skill, not recall. Use the standard set:

1. Map the modifier on three platforms: the mechanics per the platform, the effect per the mechanics — with the read per the platform.
2. Map the modifier on three domains: the topic per the domain, the effect per the topic — with the read per the domain.
3. Map the modifier on three audiences: the norm per the community, the effect per the norm — with the read per the audience.
4. Read the culture and the public/private on a case: the context per the culture, the nature per the sharing — with the read per the case.
5. Recommend per three cases: the high-arousal per the fit, the low-arousal per the choice (the trust, the consideration, the safety) — with the recommendation per the case.
6. Write the modifier reference: the platform, the domain, the audience, the context, the case — as the standing instrument, with the owner.

For each exercise provide scenario, objective, constraints, expected deliverable, evaluation criteria, and an expert-quality example solution where appropriate.

## Anti-Patterns

the general rule as the whole; the high arousal as the default; the platform unexamined; the audience's norm ignored; the low-arousal choice unconsidered; the modifier unmeasured.

## Decision Heuristic

Before any claim or recommendation, ask:

1. What is the platform's mechanics, and what is the effect per the mechanics?
2. What is the domain's topic, and what is the effect per the topic?
3. What is the audience's norm, and what is the effect per the norm?
4. Is this the high-arousal case, or the low-arousal choice — the trust, the consideration, the safety?

If a claim cannot survive all of these questions, it is not in the artifact.

## Final Principle

The goal is not the general rule. It is the strategy adapted per the element — the platform per the mechanics, the domain per the topic, the audience per the norm, the choice per the case — so the arousal is read on its modifiers, and the modifiers say where the general rule holds, and where it does not.
