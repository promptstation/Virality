Read the quadrants: excitement/joy (high arousal, positive valence), anxiety/anger/fear (high arousal, negative valence), calm/contentment (low arousal, positive valence), sadness/boredom (low arousal, negative valence) — the quadrant per the emotion, with the membership per the position.

Teach the angular relationships: opposites 180° apart (joy and sadness), uncorrelated 90° apart (excitement and anger), similar by adjacency (anxiety and anger) — the angle as the similarity measure, and the prediction per the angle.

Plot the mixed emotions: the excited-but-anxious state as the neighbor region, the contentment-with-unease as the quadrant's edge — the mixed state per the position, with the read per the angle.

Define core affect: the always-on background state beneath the named emotions (the ambient valence and arousal of the moment, before the label) — the background per the state, and its role per the transmission (the background that the content enters).
