# Step 2 — Generated Prompt (Result of the Reusable Prompt)

```
Develop a comprehensive, professional-level learning module on **“Integrated STEPPS Strategy: Auditing and Orchestrating the Six Levers”** as part of an advanced course on the science of why ideas, products, and messages spread socially. The module should how the six STEPPS levers work as a system — audit, weighting, pipeline, and measurement — so that transmission becomes a designed outcome rather than a lucky event.

The material should teach the system layer: auditing content across all six levers, weighting them by context, and running the pipeline from brief to post-mortem. Go beyond the single-lever habit (everyone runs one 'viral' tactic) and the assumption that the levers are interchangeable and examine how the levers interact, conflict, and compound in real campaigns.

Teach the STEPPS audit: score each asset 0–5 on each lever, identify the dominant lever, and flag assets that attempt all six and achieve none.

Show the weighting logic: the dominant lever is set by audience (who shares what), platform (what the surface rewards), and goal (reach, conversion, or norm change).

Build the content engine: the pipeline in which every asset enters with a named lever, a measurement plan, and a follow-up asset, and exits with a post-mortem against the lever's KPI.

Teach lever interaction: story carries emotion and currency; triggers re-activate value; visibility amplifies the observed result — and the conflicts (a utility asset does not need a story wrap; an awe campaign does not need a checklist).

Run the synthesis: a complete campaign, from brief (lever choice, weighting) through production (wrap, directing, affordances) to post-mortem (KPI versus prediction) and the next brief.

Include practical methods for working at a professional level:
* The six-lever audit scorecard (0–5 per lever, dominant lever named)
* Weighting matrix: audience × platform × goal
* The content-engine pipeline (intake, lever assignment, measurement plan, follow-up, post-mortem)
* KPI mapping per lever (currency → share rate, triggers → cued recall, emotion → arousal pretest, utility → forwarding rate, visibility → uptake, story → transmission)
* The post-mortem template: prediction versus result, per lever

Include practical exercises that require the learner to:
1. Audit a portfolio of ten assets with the six-lever scorecard; produce the dominant-lever map and the two biggest gaps.
2. Build the weighting matrix for a defined audience, platform, and goal; defend the dominant-lever choice with the audience evidence.
3. Design the content-engine pipeline: intake criteria, lever assignment, KPI mapping, follow-up rule, post-mortem template.
4. Plan a quarter's calendar balanced across levers, with each asset's lever, KPI, and follow-up named.
5. Run the full synthesis on one campaign: brief → production decisions → measurement plan → a simulated post-mortem against the prediction.
6. Write the system's learning rule: how post-mortem results change next quarter's weighting, with one worked example of a missed KPI.

For each exercise, provide the scenario, objective, constraints, expected deliverable, evaluation criteria, and an expert-quality example solution where appropriate.

Structure the material progressively, beginning with the audit and the single-lever habit, moving through weighting, the pipeline, and KPI mapping, then to a running content engine with honest post-mortems. Establish clear conceptual distinctions between **lever, dominant lever, lever weighting, and lever interaction** before showing how they interact.

Use professional terminology from marketing science, social psychology, and communication research where relevant, but explain specialized terminology in clear language. Do not make the material sound like generic AI-generated advice; the register should be that of an advanced professional training module used by content strategists, brand marketers, and communication researchers.

Research the subject using high-quality professional and academic sources where external research materially strengthens the material. Prioritize authoritative sources: Jonah Berger's research program (Journal of Consumer Research, Journal of Marketing Research, and the book 'Contagious'), the word-of-mouth and social-contagion literature, and replication studies of the six levers. Avoid relying primarily on low-quality SEO articles, generic stepps framework blogs, or unsupported “best practice” claims. Where useful, distinguish established research findings from professional conventions and informed recommendations.

Pay particular attention to the relationship between this module and principles such as **dominant-lever discipline, audience fit, KPI mapping, and post-mortem honesty**. Connect these concepts to concrete analytical and design decisions rather than discussing them only theoretically.

Create a professional framework that content strategists, brand marketers, and communication researchers could actually use in a real project. The final material should therefore function simultaneously as a learning resource, a practical reference, and a working methodology for content and communication strategy.

Throughout the module, prioritize language that is:

* Precise without being jargon-heavy
* Empirical without being statistical theater
* Confident about what the evidence establishes
* Explicit about what the evidence does not establish
* Conditional where the findings are conditional
* Practical where the decisions are practical

Where a claim depends on context (platform, audience, topic, measurement), explicitly explain the trade-off and the boundary condition rather than presenting it as an absolute rule.

End the module with **Audit completion checklist**, **Weighting decision checklist**, **Engine operation checklist**, and a **Delivery gate checklist** that a professional team can use before acting on content and communication strategy.

The final result should be comprehensive enough to serve as an advanced professional training module, but organized clearly enough that a learner can study it progressively and apply each concept in content and communication strategy.
```
