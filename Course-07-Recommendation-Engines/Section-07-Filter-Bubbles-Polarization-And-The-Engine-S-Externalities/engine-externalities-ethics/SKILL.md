# Filter Bubbles, Polarization, and the Engine's Externalities: Professional Module Builder

## Mission

Produce professional-level material on how the engine's externalities are measured — the bubble, the polarization, the well-being — and where the strategist's line sits inside them. The unit of work is a defensible artifact — a learning module, an evidence brief, a case diagnosis, or a strategy memo — in which every claim is tied to a source, a mechanism, and a boundary condition.

Optimize simultaneously for:

- empirical precision (what the canonical studies and works actually found)
- conceptual clarity (the core terms kept strictly distinct)
- causal discipline (correlational and causal claims never blurred)
- practical utility (a practitioner can act on the output the same day)
- intellectual honesty (limits and boundary conditions as visible as the findings)

Do not produce generic advice or pop-science hype. The register is that of an advanced professional training program: confident where the evidence is strong, explicitly conditional where it is not.

## Use Cases

### New professional learning module
When asked to create a full module, course unit, or workshop on this topic:
1. Establish scope: audience, depth, and which claims the module must cover.
2. Build the evidence base from primary sources and canonical works.
3. Develop the core mechanisms and diagnostic logic.
4. Add method, critique, and application sections.
5. End with exercises and the delivery checklists.
6. Deliver the complete artifact with no placeholders.

### Brief or leadership memo
When asked for a short document that must decide something:
1. State the question the document must answer in one sentence.
2. Include only findings strong enough to bear that decision, each with its mechanism and source.
3. Convert results into plain-language implications.
4. End with the specific decisions the evidence changes.
5. Cap length; cut context before cutting caveats.

### Case diagnosis
When asked to explain why a specific piece of content, campaign, or product spread (or did not):
1. Identify the operative mechanisms — never a single assumed cause.
2. Map the case onto the diagnostic framework of this skill.
3. Run the differential: which mechanisms were active, which alternatives were considered and set aside.
4. State confidence and the evidence that would revise the diagnosis.

### Strategy design
When asked to design a campaign, content system, or program around this lever:
1. Fix the objective, audience, and platform context.
2. Select the mechanisms from this skill that fit that context.
3. Specify implementation, sequencing, and the measurement plan.
4. State the boundary conditions under which the plan breaks.

## Core Output Requirements

Regardless of format:

- Every named finding or claim carries its source or mechanism in the same breath.
- Correlational and causal claims are labeled as such wherever they matter.
- Effect claims carry their metric and a plain-language rendering.
- Boundary conditions are stated as explicit “applies when / breaks down when” conditions.
- Examples use real, named studies, campaigns, or artifacts — not invented case studies.
- No padding sections, generic intro/outro filler, or advice that would read the same for any topic.

## Phase 1 — Scope and Claim Audit

### Artifact and audience
What the user actually needs (module vs. memo vs. diagnosis) and who reads it. Different readers make different cuts of the same material.

### Claim inventory
List the claims the artifact will make. For each, note the source or mechanism that supports it and whether that support is correlational, experimental, or canonical. Downgrade or cut unsupported claims — not the caveat.

### Depth budget
Match length to the decision the artifact serves. The fix for a weak document is choosing stronger claims to carry it, not adding hedges to weaker ones.

## Phase 2 — Foundations and Core Content

1. Teach the filter bubble with its measure: the exposure narrowing over the user's history (the topic spread's trend, the source spread's trend), the degrees (the narrowing that is the taste's service — the content the user values, clustered — versus the narrowing that is the opinion's cage — the viewpoint's exclusion), and the evidence's state (the studies, the measures, the debate).
2. Cover the polarization's evidence honestly: the studies of the feed's effect (the exposure, the attitude, the affect), the mixed findings (the effect's size, the population, the platform), and the distinction that matters (the exposure to the viewpoint versus the agreement with it — the bubble's mechanism versus the radicalization's claim, and the evidence for each).
3. Cover the well-being research's state: the usage's effect (the dose, the time, the population), the vulnerable population (the adolescent, the vulnerable state), the honest summary (the evidence's direction, its uncertainty, its population boundary), and the platform's welfare response (the objective's addition, the break-up of the scroll, the well-being tool).
4. Teach the platform's responses as the system's self-correction: the welfare objective in the ranking, the diversity injection, the transparency report, the harm tool — the response per externality, and the strategy's alignment with the direction (the platform's stated welfare goal is the future of the engine, and the strategy aligned with it is the strategy aligned with the future).
5. State the strategist's share: the niche farming (the narrowing's design for the brand), the outrage's design (the affect's amplification), and the vulnerable targeting (the state's exploitation) — the three strategies that own a share of the externality, the line per strategy (the value after the reaction, the exposure's breadth, the consent of the state), and the statement of the share as the professional's record, not the afterthought.

## Phase 3 — Method and Technique

- The exposure-narrowing measure: the topic and the source spread per audience segment, over time
- The evidence review per claim: the studies, the measure, the finding, the boundary — the bubble, the polarization, the well-being
- The platform-response inventory: the welfare objective, the injection, the report, the tool — per platform, with the documentation
- The share audit: the strategy's elements mapped to the externality (the niche farming, the outrage, the targeting), the line per element
- The statement of the share: the record of what the strategy owns, the line held, and the exception's rule

## Phase 4 — Analysis and Diagnosis

- Read the audience's exposure trend: the narrowing that is the service (the taste's cluster) versus the narrowing that is the cage (the viewpoint's exclusion) — the degree the measure shows, and the strategy's position in it.
- Separate the exposure from the agreement in the polarization claim: the audience's exposure to the viewpoint, and the audience's shift — the two measures, and the strategy's claim bounded by the one it supports.
- Check the well-being boundary: the population the evidence covers, the dose, the platform — the claim's boundary, and the strategy's population inside or outside it.
- Audit the strategy's share: the elements that own the externality (the farming, the outrage, the targeting) — the line per element, and the violation, if any, named.

## Phase 5 — Application and Design

- Measure the audience's exposure as the standing read: the spread per segment, the trend, the degree — the bubble's measure, the strategy's instrument.
- Align with the welfare direction: the platform's stated welfare goal as the engine's future, and the strategy's alignment statement (the design that survives the welfare's arrival) — the alignment as the strategy's durability.
- Hold the line per element: the farming's breadth (the exposure's floor), the outrage's value (the reaction's source), the targeting's consent (the state's protection) — the line as the design constraint, not the review's footnote.
- State the share as the record: the document of what the strategy owns, the line held, the exception — the professional's standing, written.

## Phase 6 — Ethics and Boundary Conditions

- The niche farming's line is the breadth: the exposure floor the strategy maintains (the viewpoint's access, the source's spread) — the narrowing as the service, not the cage.
- The outrage's design line is the value: the reaction the content earns from its substance, versus the reaction it manufactures from the provocation — the negative signal and the retention's refund as the detectors.
- The vulnerable targeting's line is the consent: the state's protection (the adolescent, the vulnerable moment) as the exclusion, stated — the targeting that excludes the state, by policy, not by accident.

## Phase 7 — Exercises and Assessment

For learning artifacts, include exercises that force the diagnosis skill, not recall. Use the standard set:

1. Measure the exposure narrowing for an audience segment: the topic and the source spread over time — with the degree (the service versus the cage) and the strategy's position.
2. Review the polarization evidence for a claim: the studies, the measure, the finding, the boundary — with the exposure-versus-agreement separation.
3. Summarize the well-being evidence for a population: the direction, the uncertainty, the boundary — and the strategy's population's position in it.
4. Inventory the platform's welfare responses: the objective, the injection, the report, the tool — per platform, with the documentation and the strategy's alignment statement.
5. Run the share audit on a strategy: the farming, the outrage, the targeting — the line per element, and the violation, if any, named and corrected.
6. Write the statement of the share: the record of what the strategy owns, the line held, the exception's rule — as the professional's standing document.

For each exercise provide scenario, objective, constraints, expected deliverable, evaluation criteria, and an expert-quality example solution where appropriate.

## Phase 8 — Quality Gate and Delivery

Before delivering, run the delivery checklists. The artifact fails the gate on any “no.”

### Exposure measure
- The spread is measured per segment
- The degree is diagnosed
- The strategy's position is stated

### Evidence review
- The studies are per claim
- The boundary is per finding
- The exposure-agreement is separated

### Welfare alignment
- The platform's response is inventoried
- The alignment is stated
- The design survives the welfare's arrival

### Delivery gate
- The share audit ran
- The line is per element
- The statement is written

## Anti-Patterns

the claim without the measure; the generalization past the boundary; the exposure read as the agreement; the welfare dismissed; the farming without the breadth; the outrage without the value; the targeting without the consent.

## Decision Heuristic

Before any claim or recommendation, ask:

1. What does the exposure measure show, and which degree is it?
2. Does the claim rest on the exposure or on the agreement, and which one is measured?
3. What population and dose does the well-being evidence cover, and is the strategy's population inside it?
4. Where is the strategy relative to the platform's welfare direction?
5. What does the strategy own of the externality, and is the share written?

If a claim cannot survive all of these questions, it is not in the artifact.

## Final Principle

The goal is not to defend the engine, or to condemn it. It is to measure what it does, to align with where it is going, and to state — in writing — what share of the cost the strategy owns, and the line it will not cross.
